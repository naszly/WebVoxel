Game controls

  • Click once to lock cursor; press E to unlock
  • Mouse: look around
  • Left click: remove voxel
  • Right click: place voxel
  • W/A/S/D: move
  • Space: jump (or up in god mode)
  • C: down in god mode
  • Shift: sprint
  • G: gravity/god mode toggle
